## What XBRL is (slides 3-4)
- eXtensible Business Reporting Language. A way for firms to report financial data in a structured, machine-readable format.

## Tags (slides 5-8)
- A tag is a standard label put on one piece of financial data.
- Standard (taxonomy) tags come from the XBRL taxonomy and map directly to accounting standards.
- Custom (extension) tags are made by the firm for items the taxonomy doesn't cover.
- Standard tags make numbers easier to compare across firms (slide example is net PPE at Costco vs. IBM).
- Discussion question was the pros and cons of allowing custom tags. Pro is they fit the firm's business better. Con is they can be used to obfuscate and hide bad news, so the SEC watches them.

## Who runs XBRL (slide 9)
- XBRL International develops and maintains the standards.
- XBRL US promotes it in the US.
- The FASB maintains the GAAP Taxonomy (the dictionary of standard tags).
- The SEC sets the requirements for public firms, adds tags to the taxonomy and monitors custom tags.

## iXBRL (slide 11)
- Inline XBRL puts the human-readable and machine-readable versions in one document.
